One image that sums up the world's news of Thursday 26 November 2026, drawn
entirely by `generate.py` (Python + Pillow, no network, fully deterministic).

**Lead stories:** the Houthi–Saudi escalation reaches three cities — a missile
intercepted over Riyadh, a projectile at Jazan port, a drone over Jeddah —
and two tankers are struck by sea mines in the Strait of Hormuz.

**Also in the picture:** Israel strikes a Hamas official in Gaza; Ukraine hits
a Russian oil refinery; North Korea orders Switzerland's Pyongyang office to
close; a second US judge blocks Trump's birthright citizenship order;
BepiColombo reaches Mercury orbit after eight years; Gukesh–Sindarov game 2 is
drawn in 20 moves; 15-year-old Sooryavanshi smashes 204 off 53 balls; and a
fuel tanker crash in Cairo kills more than 30 people.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. Running `generate.py` twice produces byte-identical
images.
